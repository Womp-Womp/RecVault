Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

RecVault is an Android app that records audio on your phone and backs the recordings up to your own cloud storage. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data.

The short version

RecVault has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer never receives your recordings or any information about you. Your recordings go from your phone to your own Google Drive, and nowhere else.

What RecVault accesses

  • Microphone: only while you have recording switched on. A notification is shown the whole time.
  • Your recordings: saved on your phone in Recordings/RecVault. RecVault never deletes or edits them.
  • Google Drive (scope drive.file): RecVault can create files and see only the files it created, in a folder called RecVault. It cannot see, read or change anything else in your Drive.
  • Microsoft OneDrive (if enabled, scope Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder): limited to RecVault's own app folder.
  • Notifications: to show recording status and backup alarms.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Recording files and their names go to your Google Drive (and your OneDrive, if you turn it on), signed in as you.
  • Sign-in is handled by Google (and Microsoft). RecVault keeps the resulting access permission on your phone only.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. RecVault contains no advertising or analytics code.

Google user data

RecVault's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to store your own recordings in your own Drive. It is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not read by humans.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your recordings stay on your phone and in your cloud folder until you delete them. Uninstalling RecVault does not delete them. To revoke RecVault's access to Google Drive, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove RecVault.

Recording other people

Laws about recording conversations differ by place. Some require everyone's consent. You are responsible for using RecVault lawfully.

Children

RecVault is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data about anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Womp-Womp/RecVault/issues.
